## What each game's gameplay tests cover

**01 Atom Builder**
- build all 20 elements with the buttons: names, mass numbers, charge, shells and discoveries are right
- ions, unstable nuclei and the remove buttons
- every molecule in the list can be built by dragging bonds, and is named with the right mass
- tapping an atom undoes its newest bond; flicking it off the edge removes it
- a full atom refuses a fourth bond

**02 Balance Scale**
- all ten mystery-box levels can be solved by tapping, and x is right
- unbalanced moves show ≠ and the scale tilts; Undo puts things back
- every seesaw level can be balanced by tapping blocks and pegs, and torque math is shown right

**03 Bridge Builder**
- a road with nothing holding it up sags and breaks; the same span with triangles holds (every level)
- stronger materials matter: a wood truss that fails the heaviest truck holds in steel
- the same bridge gives the same result and the same stretch on a different screen size
- building with real drags and taps: beams, materials, too long, underground, erase, undo, clear, budget
- Stop during a run puts everything back; Next changes the level

**04 Cannon Launch**
- the panel formulas match independent projectile math on every planet
- a fired ball follows the parabola exactly and lands at R (unless it hits something)
- aiming by dragging: the cannon points at the finger, a longer pull is faster, letting go fires
- levels 1-12 can all be won by aiming with the formula; the level advances each time
- a ball on a slow (20 fps) screen still hits the wall instead of passing through it
- path toggle, next level and the planet keys

**05 Chance Machine**
- 1,000 balls make a binomial bell curve; average and chart agree
- sliding sideways tilts the machine; a tilted machine averages 10·p
- dice: 1, 2 and 3 dice follow the exact sum distributions
- coins: 1,000 flips land near 50% heads; streaks and clear work

**06 Circuit Lab**
- closing the starter circuit lights the bulb; the meter agrees with Ohm's law
- series, parallel, three bulbs, fan and switch challenges can all be completed with real drags
- battery and resistor values step when tapped again; Ohm's law holds with a resistor
- short circuits warn, too much power pops a bulb, broken and open circuits read zero, nothing shows NaN

**07 Bubble Pop**
- bubbles float up; a tap on a bubble pops it, a tap just outside does not
- ABC, 123 and Animals modes; ten pops earn a star; rapid taps all count

**08 Magic Paint**
- a stroke paints the picked colour exactly under the finger, right up to every edge
- brush sizes, eraser, stamps, mirror, undo and new paper
- a stroke that leaves the paper and comes back stays one continuous line, and the picture survives a resize

**09 Hungry Monster**
- each level: feed exactly the number asked for (taps and drags), get a star and a new round
- food dropped anywhere but the monster goes back to the tray and is never stranded

**10 Shape Sorter**
- every shape fits only its own hole, whatever its tilt; a full board gives a star and a new round
- keyboard: pick a shape with Enter, choose its home, Enter again

**11 Rainbow Piano**
- all eight keys play the right C-major notes (equal temperament, A4 = 440 Hz)
- Learn a song: following the glowing key plays Twinkle Twinkle to the end for a star
- Copy me: listen, repeat the notes, and the tune grows; a wrong note replays it
- sliding a finger across the keys plays each one (glissando); switching modes leaves no key stuck lit

**12 Word Builder**
- every word in both lists is a real lowercase word with a picture
- spelling rounds in both lengths: right letters fill the slots, wrong ones shake, two misses show a hint
- the picture button lights the next letter; the keyboard can spell

**13 Firework Sky**
- a tap launches a rocket that bursts into a named colour, shape or number
- two hundred frantic taps and scribbles never grow sparks or rockets without limit

**14 Number Forge**
- fifteen climbing levels: every puzzle is solvable with all its cards, the one-line formula is right
- Make 24: every deal can make 24, and playing the solution wins
- uneven division, negative answers and huge powers are refused with a hint; Undo, Start over and Hint work

**15 Potion Lab**
- twelve levels: the target can always be poured exactly by tapping bottles; the beaker shows the right sums
- too much warns, Undo and Empty fix it

**16 Shape Lab**
- dragging corners: names, area, perimeter and the angle sum match independent geometry
- "Make it perfect" gives regular polygons for 3 to 8 corners
- Pythagoras: dragging the legs finds 3-4-5 and 6-8-10; the squares add up
- circle: C = 2πr and A = πr² for every radius; unrolling measures the circumference
- unit circle: cos, sin and tan at every special angle, with exact forms

**17 Graph Rider**
- every family: the target track is reachable on the control grid, and riding it collects every star
- missing a star fails the ride with a hint; slope and area during the ride are right
- dragging the track and its pink dots changes the equation; Growth has a dot for a and one for r
- extreme settings never produce NaN or Infinity on screen

**18 Math Art**
- sunflower: sliding changes the angle and seed count; the golden-angle button finds 137.5° for one star
- spirograph: the star has R / gcd(R, r) points for every gear and ring
- fractal tree: more and fewer levels, and the counts follow 2^(n-1) and 2^n - 1
- Mandelbrot: tapping zooms in and classifies the tapped point correctly
- arrow keys change the art in every mode

**19 Laser Lab**
- all six puzzles can be solved by tapping, and every bounce obeys angle in = angle out
- the prism fans white light out: violet bends most, red least, and red matches Snell through a 60° prism
- free play: glass turns, bends red light by Snell's law and lets it out parallel; trapped light only past the critical angle
- pieces run out, come back when dragged off the board, and fixed pieces cannot be moved

**20 Physics Lab**
- falling: g is 9.8 m/s² on Earth and 1.62 m/s² on the Moon, and height energy turns into moving energy
- a rubber ball bounces back to about e² of its drop height (restitution 0.82)
- gravity menu: space floats, upside down falls up; Freeze stops time; Slow-mo slows it
- every scene loads and runs for three seconds with nothing escaping or turning into NaN
- every item in the Add menu can be added with a tap and settles without errors
- grab, pin, boom, erase, draw and ramp tools all work with the mouse
- all five puzzles can be solved by drawing ramps, then Go
- puzzles: only ramp and erase work, a miss resets the ball, Try again works, and a resize keeps the ramps
- if the physics engine cannot load, the child sees a message and Try again really retries

**21 Prime Garden**

- tapping 1, a wilted number or a composite first all behave sensibly
- factor trees: splitting until every leaf is prime gives the true prime factorisation
- GCD and LCM are right for every pair, and GCD × LCM = a × b

**22 Puzzle Box**

- Number grids 4×4 and 6×6 always have one solution; clashes turn red; Erase works; solving wins

**23 Robo Code**

**24 Element Box**

**25 Space Orbits**

- a tap makes a circular orbit; black hole, fast, names, paths, clear, planet menu and facts all work

**26 Splash Lab**
